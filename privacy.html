FretCue Privacy Policy

Good Software · Last updated: 30 Oct 2026

FretCue is a guitar practice plugin for Even Realities G2 glasses. It is made by Good Software. This policy explains what data the app handles.

What we collect

FretCue does not require an account inside the plugin, does not request location, microphone, camera, or photo-library permissions, and does not send practice data to Good Software servers.

  • Practice sets and preferences you create stay on your device (local storage in the Even Realities app WebView).
  • Distribution, install, and crash telemetry may be handled by Even Realities as part of Even Hub — see their policies for the Even Realities app and Hub.

Network

The current FretCue build does not call third-party APIs. If a future version needs network access, we will update this policy and the Even Hub permission list before shipping.
